The OpenMP custom reduction worked best for one thread. The builtin reduction works
best for 2-4 threads. the custom reduction pulled through again for 5-16 threads
again.

For a single thread, custom OpenMP has less overhead since it avoids a lot of the
inherent costs associated with the builtin method's reduction mechanism.

For 2-4 threads, the builtin is optimal because there is more optimization from the
parallel work than there is overhead from the reduction.

For 5-16 threads, the custom pulls back ahead because the reduction can cause more
conflict than time efficiency when we exceed 4 threads. The scope locking of the
custom OpenMP scales better here.
